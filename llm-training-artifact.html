Training Large Language Models

Process and Resource Costs of Generative AI Systems

Artifact Overview

This artifact illustrates the training lifecycle of modern Large Language Models (LLMs), including GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, LLaMA, and Grok. The presentation highlights the major stages of development, including data collection, preprocessing, tokenization, pre-training, fine-tuning, evaluation, and deployment.

It also demonstrates the significant resources required to train these models, including massive datasets, GPU/TPU clusters, energy consumption, cloud infrastructure, engineering effort, and months of training time.

Key Concepts Covered

  • Data collection and preparation for LLM training
  • Tokenization and model architecture design
  • Pre-training and fine-tuning processes
  • Evaluation, alignment, and safety testing
  • Deployment and continuous improvement
  • Compute, energy, time, and financial resource costs

Design Rationale

The artifact uses flowcharts, icons, model examples, and visual comparisons to make complex AI infrastructure concepts easier to understand. The design is intended to be clear for both technical and non-technical audiences while showing the high level of investment required to develop large-scale generative AI systems.
