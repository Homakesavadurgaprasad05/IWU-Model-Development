Explainable AI (XAI)

Building Trust Through Transparency

Project Overview

This project explores Explainable AI (XAI) and the challenges associated with understanding decisions made by large language models such as GPT, Claude, Gemini, and LLaMA. As AI systems become increasingly integrated into healthcare, finance, legal services, and other critical industries, transparency and accountability have become essential requirements.

The presentation examines the primary obstacles to explainability, including model complexity, hidden reasoning, data bias, and regulatory concerns. It also highlights current industry techniques used to improve transparency and discusses how validation processes and performance metrics contribute to trustworthy AI systems.

Classification Process

The information in this artifact was organized into four major categories: Explainable AI fundamentals, challenges in explainability, industry solutions, and validation and performance metrics. This structure was selected to clearly demonstrate the relationship between AI transparency, reliability, and accountability.

Organizing the content into these categories helped illustrate how explainability challenges create the need for industry solutions and how validation metrics are used to evaluate the effectiveness of those solutions.

Reflection on Learning

Through this project, I gained a deeper understanding of the challenges involved in explaining modern AI systems. I learned that explainability extends beyond simply understanding model outputs and includes fairness, transparency, accountability, and safety.

Researching the approaches used by OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Anthropic helped me understand how leading organizations are addressing transparency concerns while continuing to advance AI capabilities. I also learned how validation techniques and performance metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, hallucination rate, and human evaluation contribute to trustworthy AI development.
